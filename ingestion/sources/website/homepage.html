Welcome to WCE Sangli

Established in 1947, Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli, stands as a beacon of excellence in engineering education. As a premier autonomous institution affiliated with Shivaji University, we seamlessly blend tradition with innovation to create a holistic learning environment. With a focus on academic rigor, cutting-edge research, state-of-the-art facilities, and strong industry collaborations, WCE empowers students to excel academically, develop critical skills, and contribute meaningfully to a rapidly evolving global landscape.

  • Over 75 years of excellence in engineering education.
  • Autonomous institution affiliated with Shivaji University.
  • Accredited by NBA and NAAC with top rankings.
  • State-of-the-art labs, libraries, and research facilities.
  • Robust industry collaborations and placement opportunities.

The Walchand Legacy: From Foundation to Excellence

"Celebrating Decades of Academic Brilliance and Transformational Impact"

The legacy of WCE is built on a foundation of visionary educators who established rigorous academic standards and nurtured a culture of excellence. Over the decades, the college has evolved to embrace cutting-edge technology and innovative teaching methodologies, ensuring relevance in a rapidly changing world. From the pioneering efforts of its founding faculty to its global impact today, Walchand College of Engineering exemplifies the perfect blend of tradition and modernity.

1947

The foundation stone was laid by the Late Shri Dhondumama Sathe with a single program leading to a B.E. (Civil) degree.

1955

The college was renamed after Seth Walchand Hirachand as Walchand College of Engineering, followed by the commencement of B.E. (Mechanical) and B.E. (Electrical) along with three Diploma programs; Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical.

1965

In 1956, the inception of the Administrative Council marked a pivotal moment. Over the subsequent decade, this council, in collaboration with industry leaders, steered the operations, ushering in a distinctive paradigm within the realm of education. Distinguished figures from Walchand Industries, such as WIL, HCC, IHP, and others, offered commendable leadership, steering the development of the college through an administrative council.

1970

Walchand College excelled. Eminent figures, such as Dr. Radhakrishnan, Karmaveer Bhaurao Patil, and Yashwantrao Chavan graced Walchand College with their presence from 1947 to 1970.

1971

WCE stands out for its distinguished faculty, known for its exceptional proficiency, dedication, and commitment to providing high-quality education and guidance to students. Under their leadership, the college introduced Postgraduate programs and established a research center, marking a historic milestone as the first center within Shivaji University. This legacy of academic excellence has endured for four decades.

1977

The increase of government aid marked a pivotal moment for WCE, as it became the inaugural private engineering college to receive such support.

2003

Achieving a significant milestone, WCE proudly secured NBA Accreditation for five Undergraduate Programs, marking a pioneering achievement as the first institution within Shivaji University to attain this prestigious accreditation.

2005

WCE enthusiastically participated in the World Bank-sponsored TEQIP Scheme, marking the initiation of a series of funding in two decades and sponsorships from various agencies dedicated to the advancement of institute development and research at WCE. Notable sponsors include RGSTC, DST, MPCB, SGRF, and Sakal, among others.

2007

The college achieved autonomy status, marking a historic milestone as the first private engineering institution to attain such recognition.

2008

The college enhanced its facilities with a world-class library, spanning 50,000 sq. ft. Generously funded by the “Walchand Hirachand Memorial Trust,” it’s now named the “Ajit Gulabchand Central Library.” This expansive space houses a comprehensive collection of reading materials.

2021

WCE maintains a robust placement record, exemplified by the remarkable highest package of 1.3 Crore PA by Saurabh Pukale. It stands as the unique engineering college providing such exceptional placements. Notably, the college celebrated its Platinum Jubilee in the same year, symbolizing a rich legacy spanning 75 years. The industry’s trust in our skilled graduates is a testament to our unwavering commitment to both employability and academic excellence.

2023

The influence of WEC’s education is evident in the accomplishments of our students and alumni. WCE boasts a remarkable network of more than 25,000 alumni, creating a global influence. Among them, our esteemed alumna, Ms. Leena Nair, stands out as the CHANEL Global CEO based in London, England, ranking among the top 10 Indian-origin CEOs globally.
